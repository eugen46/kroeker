8 May 2026 — Kazakhstan

Official response received from the branch of NAO «State Corporation „Government for Citizens"» for the North Kazakhstan Region (Petropavlovsk). The existence of birth registration record No. 13 dated 14.04.1950 in the name of Kröker Vladimir has been confirmed. Issuance of a full copy of the record was refused (classified as for internal service use). It was confirmed that in the original entry, the father's field contained the name «Vasily», which was later replaced based on the mother's marriage of 26.07.1952. The search for the biological father through the civil registry is exhausted — continuing in other sources.
