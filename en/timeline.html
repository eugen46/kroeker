Timeline

  1. ~1820–1860
    Kroeker ancestors live in West Prussia. Migration to Volhynia, Ukraine.
  2. 1864
    Johann Kroeker born 15 November in Volhynia (exact village unknown).
  3. 1877 / 1884
    Emma Gotlibovna Schulz born in Wiktorowka, Baryschiw district, Kyiv Oblast.
  4. 1917
    Lydia Iwanovna Kroeker born 17.04.1917 in Schelesnjak.
  5. 1924
    Emma Iwanovna Kroeker born 05.05.1924, Uwarowka/Alexandrowka, Pulin district.
  6. ~1935
    Family forcibly resettled to Peski village, Kharkiv Oblast. Famine 1932–1933.
  7. 1935
    Lydia marries Edmund Dreger on 5 June 1935 in Peski. Son Leo born 8 June 1937.
  8. 1941
    Edmund Dreger (Lydia's husband) deported (EWZ data).
  9. 1941
    October: deportation from Peski, Dvurechansky district, Kharkiv Oblast to Sennoje, North Kazakhstan Oblast.
  10. 1943
    Lydia leaves Ukraine with German forces.
  11. 1944
    28 April: Lydia and Leo receive German citizenship in Hermannsbad.
  12. 1950
    8 April: Vladimir born in Sennoje. Father in original record only: “Wassili” (surname unknown).
  13. 1952
    Emma marries Nikolaj Usachew (stepfather).
  14. 1955
    16 December: release from special settlement regime.
  15. 1987
    2 May: Emma Iwanovna dies in Dubowskoje, Rostov Oblast, aged 62.
  16. 1993
    Emma Iwanovna officially rehabilitated (Republic of Kazakhstan).
  17. 2019
    Eugen moves to Germany. Name changed to Eugen Usachew.
  18. 2023
    6 August: Vladimir Usachew dies in Shakhty.
  19. 2026
    April: EWZ documents received from MHSBC Canada (Film B019, Frames 1340–1353).
