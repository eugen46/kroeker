Kroeker Family History and Genealogy

This website documents the Kroeker family history from Prussia to Volhynia, Kazakhstan and Germany. It collects genealogy notes, family branches, archival references and migration routes connected with the Kroeker, Schulz, Dreger and Fröhlich families.

The research focuses on Mennonite and Volhynian German roots, German settlers in Kazakhstan, EWZ records, family memories and archive requests related to West Prussia, Wolhynien, Ukraine, North Kazakhstan and Germany.

About This Project

This website documents the history of the Kroeker family — a Mennonite family with roots in West Prussia that passed through Volhynia (Ukraine) and Kazakhstan before arriving in Germany.

The Family Journey

West Prussia (before 1864) → Volhynia / Ukraine (1864–1941) → Kazakhstan, Sennoje (1941–1955) → Russia, Shakhty (1955–2019) → Germany, Berlin (from 2019)

West Prussia→Volhynia→Peski→Sennoje→Shakhty→Berlin

❓ Main Research Question

Who is the biological father of Vladimir Nikolaevich Usachew (born 08.04.1950, Sennoje, North Kazakhstan)? In the original birth record the father is listed only as “Wassili” — based on the words of Emma Iwanovna herself. Whether he bore the surname Kröker is not documented. His identity is the main question of the research.

Family Surnames and Branches

Kroeker / Kröker Family

The Kroeker family line is connected with West Prussia, Volhynia and later Kazakhstan and Germany. Key names include Johann Kroeker / Johann Kröker and Emma Iwanowna Kroeker.

Schulz Family from Wolhynien

The Schulz branch includes Gottlieb Schulz and Emma Gotlibovna Schulz from Volhynia. This branch is important for searches around Schulz Wolhynien genealogy and Volhynian Germans.
