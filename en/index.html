Why this matters

Why the archive was created

The truth that was kept silent in childhood

“When I was a child, people tried not to tell me about the past. Names, origins and painful facts were hidden because a Russian surname seemed like protection and a chance for a quieter life. I understand that choice — it was made for safety. But today I want to restore the truth carefully, through documents and without invention.”

Eugen Usachev · archive author

Purpose of the project

Not to blame relatives, but to understand why the past had to be hidden and to preserve a documented history for future generations.

  • Separate documents from assumptions
  • Explain the silence and the change of surname
  • Restore people’s names and journeys
