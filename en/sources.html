Documentary foundation

Sources and licences

The principal facts are based on archival files, certificates and registers. Unconfirmed information is identified as a hypothesis.

Archival sources

  • Archival certificate FL-220 dated 15 July 2026 — confirms the birth record of Vladimir Vasilyevich Kreker.
  • Personal file no. 1229 for special settler Emma Ivanovna Kreker, 28 pages — Sectoral State Archive of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.
  • Personal file for special settler Emma Gottliebovna Frelich — collection 161, inventory 1, file 866.
  • Rehabilitation certificate for E. I. Kreker and E. G. Frelich — Prosecutor General’s Office of Kazakhstan, 27 September 2016.
  • EWZ50 Film B019, frames 1340–1364 — Mennonite Heritage and Archives Society of BC, Canada.
  • EWZ file R 9361-IV/18702: Edmund and Lydia Dreger, née Kroeker, EWZ no. 906134 — Bundesarchiv Berlin-Lichterfelde; reviewed on 5 August 2026.
  • Household registers of the Sennoye village council, 1948–1953 — North Kazakhstan State Archive, collection 23.

Archival scans

Complete scans of personal files are not published. Receiving an archival copy does not itself grant permission for public distribution. Verified facts are summarized with the document’s origin identified.
